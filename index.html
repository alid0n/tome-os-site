What's inside

De-googled Android: no Google apps, no tracking, and a phone shaped around talking to people.

Telemancer

Hold a favorite to telemance them: they hear you live, like a radio. Set your status to Available, Busy or Away, and if someone is away your words arrive as a Scroll they can play later.

Wisp runs your phone

Ask in words or out loud and Wisp does it: adds events to your calendar, sends a text at 8am, sets timers, turns on the flashlight, and circles the setting you're looking for. Everything it does can be undone.

"text Mom happy birthday at 8am"

AI that stays on your phone

Wisp's brain can run entirely on the phone, offline and private, so what you ask never leaves it. Or bring your own key for a cloud model when you want one.

Make it yours with Runes

Runes change Home's colors, letters, transitions and blocks, and fit in a message to share. Ask Wisp to "make Home look like a forest" and try it before you keep it.

Spells, tiny apps from a sentence

Ask Wisp for a water tracker, a tally or a checklist and it makes one. Spells can't touch the internet or your files.

Bard, music that stays yours

A music player for the songs already on your phone, with smart play, playlists and tags. It has no internet access at all.

Private by default

De-googled from the start: no Google apps, no Google tracking, and none of the hidden background check-ins that phones make out of the box.

Command bar

Type to do almost anything, from messaging a friend to changing your status. Every setting is in reach, and your tabs sit on the right edge where your thumb is.

@tim on my way

Maps that work offline

CoMaps comes built in: download your area once and find your way with no signal and no one tracking where you go.

You decide who reaches you

Only contacts you approve can talk to you live. When you're Busy, people are asked before they telemance you. Quiet hours turn everything into Scrolls overnight.

Encrypted end to end

Live talk and voice messages are locked on your phone and unlocked only on theirs. Nobody in between can listen, including us.

Channels for your groups

Hold to talk to the whole family or crew at once. Admins can pin announcements, choose who can talk, and decide whether messages arrive silently or can be replayed.

Talk with friends on any phone

Your friends don't need Tome OS. With the Telemancer app, they can talk live with you from any Android phone.

How to install

Install from your browser with the web installer: no tools to set up. Installing Tome OS erases the phone, so back up anything you want to keep first.

  1. Turn on OEM unlocking

    In Developer options on the phone. Phones locked to a carrier, like Verizon Pixels, can't be unlocked.

  2. Download the factory image

    Get the Factory image for your phone from the table above. Big ones come in parts: tap Download all to get every part at once. If your browser asks, allow multiple downloads. Don't unzip them.

  3. Start Tome

    Open the web installer in Chrome or Edge, plug the phone in and press Start Tome. It unlocks the phone, installs Tome OS and locks it again with Tome OS's own key, so it's as secure as stock.

  4. Changed your mind?

    You can always go back to stock Android with Google's Android Flash Tool at flash.android.com.
