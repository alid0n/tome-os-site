Hold. Talk. Heard.

Telemancer feels like the phone app you already know, with a talk button that reaches your friends instantly.

Private by design

Your voice is for the people you choose, and nobody else.

Only people you know

Two phones connect only when each has the other's number saved, or when you share an invite yourself.

Encrypted end to end

Your voice, texts and calls are locked on your phone and unlocked only on theirs. Nobody in between can listen, including us.

Kept on your phone

Your contacts and conversations stay on your phone. Back them up with 12 recovery words that only you hold.

You decide who reaches you

Choose who can talk to you live, set quiet hours, and let everyone else's words wait as Scrolls.

Free, every day

Texts, Scrolls and status are always free. Live talk and voice calls are free for 30 minutes a day, and new phones start with two weeks unlimited.
