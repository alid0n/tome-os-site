Install Tome OS

Install Tome OS on a Google Pixel right from this page, then lock the phone so it's as secure as when it came from Google. You need a computer (or another Android phone) with Chrome or Edge, and a USB cable.

This browser can't talk to phones over USB. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS computer, or in Chrome on an Android phone.

Before you start

  • Turn on OEM unlockingOn the Pixel: Settings › About phone › tap Build number seven times. Then Settings › System › Developer options › turn on OEM unlocking. The phone needs to have been online once; on carrier-locked phones, like Verizon Pixels, it stays greyed out and Tome OS can't be installed.
  • Back up anything you want to keepInstalling erases everything on the phone: photos, messages, apps and accounts.
  • Charge the phoneAbove 50% is plenty. Use a good USB cable, plugged straight into the computer (not through a hub).

On Windows: if the browser can't see the phone in fastboot mode, install Google's USB driver, then unplug the phone and plug it back in.

1. Get Tome OS for your phone

Download the Factory image for your phone from the download table or the releases page. It's called tome-os-<version>-<phone>-factory.zip. Don't unzip it. Then choose it here. If it came in parts (ending in .part1, .part2), choose all the parts together.

2. Connect the phone and start

  1. Put the phone in fastboot mode

    Turn the phone off. Hold Volume Down, and while holding it, press and hold Power until a screen with an Android lying on its back appears ("Fastboot Mode"). Then plug it into this computer.

  2. Press Start Tome

    Pick the phone when the browser asks. From there it runs by itself: it unlocks the phone, installs Tome OS, adds Tome OS's key and locks the phone again. It only stops twice, when the phone asks you to confirm with its buttons. Keep this page open and the phone plugged in until it says it's finished (about 10 minutes).

Leave the phone on the fastboot screen and plugged in.

Starting Tome will wipe and reset the device

Everything on the phone is erased and replaced with Tome OS. This can't be undone.

Getting ready…

Waiting for the phone to come back. If the phone shows the fastboot screen but nothing happens here, choose Reconnect and pick the phone again.

Tome OS is installed and the phone is locked. It now restarts into Tome OS. The first start takes a few minutes. You can unplug it.

Each time it starts, the phone shows a yellow notice that it's running a different operating system. That's normal for a locked phone with an operating system other than Google's: it means the phone checked Tome OS with Tome OS's key before starting it.

Why lock the phone again?

Unlocking lets the phone take a new operating system. Locking it again, with Tome OS's own key added, means it only starts Tome OS as we signed it. If anyone changes the system, even with the phone in their hands, it refuses to start. That's the same protection the phone had from Google. Updates still install as normal.

To go back to stock Android at any time, use Google's Android Flash Tool and choose Lock bootloader and Wipe device.
