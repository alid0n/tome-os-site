Back up your phone first

Installing wipes the phone clean, like a factory reset. Choose what to keep, and this page saves it to this computer now and moves it back to the phone once Tome OS is installed.

What to keep

Untick anything you don't need. The numbers show once the phone is connected.

    1. Turn on USB debugging on the phoneSettings › About phone, tap Build number 7 times. Then Settings › System › Developer options, turn on USB debugging.
    2. Plug it in, connect, and tap Allow on the phone
    3. Choose where to save it
    Look at your phoneTap Allow

    It asks "Allow USB debugging?". Tick Always allow from this computer, then tap Allow. If nothing shows, unlock the phone's screen.

    Starting…

    Keep the phone plugged in until it says it's saved.

    What this page doesn't copy. Do these on the phone yourself:

    • Messages, contacts and calendar aren't moved by this version of the installer. Contacts and calendars kept in an online account come back when you sign in to it. Otherwise export them to a file in the phone's Downloads folder, and that file is saved with your downloads.
    • Chats in WhatsApp, Signal and others. Each has a backup option in its own settings.
    • Apps and what's inside them, like game progress and logins. Reinstall them on Tome OS and sign in again.
    • Sign-in codes in authenticator apps. Move them to another device first, or you could get locked out of your accounts.
    Stuck? Get help with this step

    Phone not in the list? Check USB debugging is on and the phone is unlocked. Unplug it and plug it back in.

    Says the phone is in use? Another program has it, like Android Studio or adb. Close it, or run adb kill-server, then press Connect again.

    "Allow USB debugging?" never shows? Unlock the phone. If it still doesn't show, go to Developer options, tap Revoke USB debugging authorizations, then connect again.

    Rather copy files yourself? Plug the phone in, pull down from the top of its screen, tap the USB notification and choose File transfer. Then copy the DCIM, Pictures, Download, Documents and Music folders to this computer.

    Let your phone accept Tome OS

    Phones only take a new operating system once you switch on a setting called OEM unlocking. It's hidden, so here's the trick. Try it on the practice phone, then do the same on your real phone.

    1. Open Settings › About phone
    2. Tap Build number 7 times
    3. Go back to Settings › System › Developer options
    4. Turn on OEM unlocking

    Connect the phone to Wi-Fi, wait a minute, then look again. If it stays grey, the phone was locked by the carrier it was bought from (Verizon phones usually are), and Tome OS can't be installed on it.

    Stuck? Get help with this step

    Asked for your PIN? That's normal. Enter the phone's screen lock.

    Can't find Developer options? Go back to About phone and tap Build number a few more times.

    Phone already runs another custom OS, like LineageOS? OEM unlocking is already on. Skip this step.

    Put the phone in install mode

    Watch the practice phone, then do the same on yours. It's two buttons held together.

    1. Turn the phone offHold Power and Volume Up together, then tap Power off.
    2. Hold Volume Down, then also hold Power
    3. Let go when you see an Android lying on its back
    4. Plug it in with the USB cable
    Stuck? Get help with this step

    Phone started normally instead? You let go too early. Turn it off and try again, holding both buttons until the Android appears.

    Screen says "Fastboot Mode" in red? That's exactly right.

    On Windows, if the next step can't find the phone, install Google's USB driver, then unplug the phone and plug it back in.

    Install Tome OS

    Press the button, then choose your phone in the box your browser shows. After that it runs by itself. It only needs you twice, to press buttons on the phone, and this page shows you which.

    Waiting for the download to finish:

    One thing first on Windows. Part way through, the phone switches to a second install screen, and Windows can't see it there without Google's USB driver. It takes about two minutes.

    Everything on the phone is erased and replaced with Tome OS. This can't be undone.

    1. Connect to the phone
    2. Unlock it to accept Tome OS
    3. Install Tome OS
    4. Add Tome OS's key
    5. Check Tome OS starts
    6. Lock it again so it stays secure
    Watch your phone Wait for the Tome welcome screen

    It's starting Tome OS for the first time, which takes up to 5 minutes. First it shows an orange warning, which is normal while it's unlocked. Don't set it up yet: locking erases it once more.

    1. Hold Power to turn the phone off first. When a menu shows, tap Power off.
    2. Once the screen is dark, hold Volume Down and Power together.
    3. Let go when the fastboot screen shows. Keep it plugged in.

    Move your files back

    Once Tome OS is set up, this page copies what you saved back to where it was: photos back in the gallery, downloads back in Downloads.

    1. Set up Tome OS on the phoneGo through the welcome screens until you reach the home screen.
    2. Turn on USB debuggingLike before: Settings › About phone, tap Build number 7 times. Then Settings › System › Developer options, turn on USB debugging.
    3. Choose your backupThe folder or zip file this page saved. Skip this if you didn't save any.
