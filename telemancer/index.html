Everything in one place

Telemancer is a full phone and messaging app with live talk at its heart, so your calls, texts and voice from each person sit together.

Live talk

Hold to talk and your friend hears you as you speak. A voice meter and a gentle buzz tell you when you're on air.

Scrolls

Voice messages for when someone is busy or offline. Each Scroll can show its words, written out on your phone, and waits up to a week.

Texts

Chat, reply to a message, and edit or unsend one you didn't mean to send.

Voice and video calls

Call one friend or a whole group. Calls ring like a normal phone call, even on the lock screen. Video calls come with Plus.

Channels

A radio channel for your group: hold to talk and everyone in it hears you. Admins can post announcements and share invite links.

Status and quiet hours

Show if you're available, busy or away. When you're busy, people are asked before they talk to you live; when you're away, their voice waits as a Scroll. Choose who may talk to you live, and set quiet hours.

Car mode

Big, simple controls for the road, and talk from your headset button without looking at the phone.

Backup with 12 words

Move to a new phone and bring your contacts, messages and settings with you, locked with recovery words only you have.

Themes

Pick a look that suits you, from the glowing Sorcerer's Orb to a warm analog field radio.

A look inside

Familiar tabs for favorites, contacts, messages, the keypad and your status.

How friends connect

Telemancer only connects people who know each other, so nobody can reach you out of the blue.

  1. Find Tome friends

    Telemancer sends each contact a hidden text carrying your public key. A phone running Telemancer keeps it only if it already has your number saved, then answers with its own.

  2. Or send an invite

    Friends who don't have Telemancer yet get an invite link by text or email. Once they install it and open the link, you're connected.

  3. Check your safety code

    Each pair of friends shares a safety code. If it matches on both phones, nobody is in the middle.

Questions

Who can hear me?

Only the friend or channel you're talking to. Your voice is encrypted on your phone and can only be unlocked on theirs. You also choose who may talk to you live: everyone you've connected with, your favorites, people you pick, or nobody.

Do my friends need Telemancer?

For live talk, Scrolls and calls, yes. Telemancer offers to send them an invite, and you both get two extra weeks of unlimited live talk when they join. Friends using Tome OS have Telemancer built in.

Does it use my texting plan?

Finding friends sends a few hidden texts, which your carrier treats as normal texts, so your plan's usual rates apply. Telemancer asks before sending any. Talk, Scrolls and calls use the internet.

What does the Telemancer server see?

The server passes sealed, encrypted pieces between phones and holds them for up to a week if a phone is off. It never sees what you say or who you are talking to. On the same Wi-Fi, phones can talk to each other directly.

What happens when my free minutes run out?

Nothing stops. Your live talk goes as Scrolls until your minutes come back at midnight, and texts and Scrolls are always free.

Why isn't it on Google Play?

Google Play only lets texting apps use text messages, and Telemancer uses hidden texts to find which of your contacts have it. Installing from this site gives you the full app.
