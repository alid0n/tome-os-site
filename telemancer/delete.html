Delete your Telemancer account

You can delete your Telemancer account, and what we keep about it, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Telemancer and tap the settings gear.
  2. Under About, tap Delete account.
  3. Read what will be removed, then tap Delete account.

It happens straight away. A Telemancer Plus subscription paid through Stripe is cancelled at the same moment. If you pay through Google Play, cancel the subscription in Google Play too (Telemancer shows you a button for it), so it doesn't renew.

What is deleted

  • On our servers: your Telemancer account key and plan, free time used and invite rewards, our records of your purchases, photos and files you sent that are still stored, your server backup, and any feedback, cancellation reasons and reports you sent us.
  • On your phone: everything Telemancer keeps, including messages, recordings, your contacts' Telemancer keys and your settings.

What is kept

  • Payment records held by Stripe or Google Play, which they keep as the law requires.
  • A scrambled identifier for the phone, so a free trial is given only once per phone. It can't be used to identify you.
  • Crash reports, which contain no personal information, for up to 90 days.
  • Sealed messages already waiting on our relay for someone else, which expire within 7 days and which we can't read.

If you no longer have the app

Your Telemancer account has no name, email address or phone number on our servers, so we can't look it up for you. An account that nobody uses for 13 months is deleted automatically, unless a paid plan is still running. To delete it sooner, install Telemancer, restore your account with your 12 recovery words, then delete it from Settings. Questions: alid0ndev@gmail.com.
