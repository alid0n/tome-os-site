Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Cancelling

You can cancel a monthly or yearly plan at any time in Telemancer: open Settings, tap your plan, then tap Cancel subscription. It stops the next renewal; you keep your plan until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you won't be charged again. You can turn it back on from the same page before then.

Refunds

  • Not what you expected? Write to us within 14 days of your first payment for a plan, or of buying lifetime, and we'll refund it in full.
  • Forgot to cancel? Write to us within 7 days of a renewal you didn't mean to keep, and we'll refund that renewal.
  • Otherwise, payments are not refunded for the unused part of a period, except where the law requires it.

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, usually within 5 to 10 business days. When a payment is refunded, its plan ends.

How to ask

Email alid0ndev@gmail.com from the address on your receipt, and say which purchase it is. We reply within 2 business days.
