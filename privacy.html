Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Telemancer is an Android app for talking with the people you trust: live voice, voice messages (Scrolls), texts, photos and files, and voice and video calls. It is made by Tome OS ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Telemancer uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your conversations are encrypted end to end. Our servers pass them along but cannot read, hear or see them.
  • Your contacts stay on your phone. We never upload your address book.
  • We keep only what the service needs to work, such as your plan and how much free time you have used, and we delete it on a schedule.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.

What stays on your phone

Your contacts, your message history, recordings, photos and files you receive, your status settings, and the turning of voice messages into text all stay on your phone. Telemancer reads your contacts only to find which of them use Telemancer and to show their names.

What passes through our servers, sealed

Messages, live voice, Scrolls, photos, files and call setup are encrypted on your phone before they leave it, with keys that only you and the people you talk with have.

  • Messages and voice pass through our relay. A message waiting for a phone that is off is kept, still encrypted, for up to 7 days, then deleted.
  • Photos and files are kept, encrypted, for 30 days so the people you sent them to can download them, then deleted.
  • Calls connect directly between phones when they can. When they can't, they pass through our call relay. Group calls go through our call server. Call audio and video are encrypted end to end and are not recorded.

What our servers keep

  • Your Telemancer key and plan: an anonymous key created on your phone, when you joined, your plan (free, trial, Plus, Family or Team) and until when, family plan members you added, and invite rewards.
  • A device check: when you first start Telemancer, Google Play Integrity confirms it is a genuine copy on a real phone. We keep a scrambled identifier for the phone so a free trial is given once per phone.
  • Free time used: on the free plan, how many minutes of live talk and calls you have used, so the daily allowance works on every phone.
  • Days you opened the app, kept for 60 days, used only to decide whether a returning subscriber is offered a discount.
  • Feedback, cancellation reasons and reports you choose to send us. A report includes the messages you choose to attach, since we cannot otherwise read them.
  • Crash reports: if Telemancer crashes, it sends where in its code it failed, with the app version, Android version and phone model. They contain no messages, contacts or account details and aren't linked to you. You can turn them off in Settings, under About.
  • An encrypted backup, if you turn backups on. It is locked with your recovery words, which we never receive.
  • Connection details: like any internet service, our relay sees the internet address a message comes from. It is kept with the sealed message, for up to 7 days, to protect the service from abuse. Messages are filed under scrambled names, and we don't keep a list of who talks with whom.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, never to us. Stripe tells us which plan was paid for, its subscription ID and when it renews, and gives us the email address and name you entered at checkout so we can send receipts and help with billing questions. Stripe's handling of your information is described in the Stripe Privacy Policy.

Other services we use

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) wakes Telemancer when a call or message arrives while the app is closed. The wake-up carries no readable content.
  • Google Play Integrity checks the app and phone are genuine, as described above.
  • Hetzner hosts our servers in Germany.
  • Text messages (SMS): to connect with a contact the first time, Telemancer may send a hidden setup text or an invite text from your phone. Your carrier's usual rates may apply.

How long we keep things

Sealed messages: up to 7 days. Sealed photos and files: 30 days. Days you opened the app: 60 days. Plan, account and payment records: while you use Telemancer, and as long as the law requires for payment records. Feedback, cancellation reasons and reports: up to 13 months. Crash reports: 90 days.

Your choices

  • You can block anyone, choose who can talk to you live, and turn off read receipts and typing indicators in the app.
  • You can ask for a copy of what we keep about you, or for it to be deleted, by writing to alid0ndev@gmail.com. Uninstalling the app deletes everything that is on your phone.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over your information, and we will honor them.

Children

Telemancer is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 is using Telemancer, write to us and we will delete what we hold.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and say so in the app or on this site before the change takes effect.
