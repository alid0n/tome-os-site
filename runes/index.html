For Tome Home

Runes

Runes are small files that change how your Home screen looks and moves. Give it new colors, a new way for tabs to move, or a little block of text that updates itself.

See the runes Share your own

Can't go online

A rune has no way to reach the internet. It can't send anything or fetch anything.

Can't see your data

Your messages, contacts, photos and files stay out of reach. A block only knows simple things, like the time and the day.

You see it first

Open this page on your Tome phone and tap Install. Tome Home shows you what the rune changes before you add it.

The gallery

Every rune here was checked by hand. On a computer, Install saves the file: send it to your phone and tap it there.

  • Day left

    Day left

    by Tome OS · version 1.0

    A block on Home that counts down the hours left in the day.

    • Block: Day left
    Install Day left Report Day left
    View code for Day left 
    {
  "tomeRune": 1,
  "id": "day-left",
  "name": "Day left",
  "author": "Tome OS",
  "version": "1.0",
  "about": "A block on Home that counts down the hours left in the day.",
  "blocks": [
    {
      "title": "Day left",
      "everyMinutes": 1,
      "script": [
        "local left = (23 - tome.hour) * 60 + (60 - tome.minute)",
        "local h, m = math.floor(left / 60), left % 60",
        "return {",
        "  text = h .. \" h \" .. m .. \" min left in \" .. tome.weekday,",
        "  lines = { \"You're \" .. string.lower(tome.mood) .. \".\" }",
        "}"
      ]
    }
  ]
}
